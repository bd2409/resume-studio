Questions agents ask

Will it say something wrong to my lead?

It starts in draft mode, so nothing goes out until you approve it. Replies never invent facts about a property, and you switch to automatic only when you're comfortable.

Is my client data safe?

Everything runs in your own accounts: your CRM, your automation tool, your AI key. I don't keep a copy of your client list.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel, kvCORE/BoldTrail, Lofty, Sierra, or a simple Google Sheet. Most accept leads by webhook or email.

Does the Fair Housing check make my ads compliant?

It flags phrases to review. It's a safety net, not legal advice, and you stay responsible for your own ads.
