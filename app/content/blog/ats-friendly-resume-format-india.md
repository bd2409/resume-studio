Most mid-size and large employers in India, and almost every multinational, now receive applications through an applicant tracking system (ATS). Job portals and company career pages parse your resume into fields, and recruiters search and filter those fields. If the parser can't read your resume properly, a recruiter may never see your experience, however strong it is.

The good news: an ATS-friendly resume is not a special trick. It is a clean, simple document that a machine and a busy recruiter can both read in seconds.

## What an ATS actually does

An applicant tracking system does three things with your resume:

1. **Parses it** into fields: name, contact details, job titles, employers, dates, education and skills.
2. **Stores and indexes it**, so recruiters can search by keywords such as "SAP FICO", "B2B SaaS sales" or "GST compliance".
3. **Ranks or filters it** against the job's requirements, in some systems, by matching skills, titles and experience.

So two things matter: the parser must read your file correctly, and your resume must contain the words a recruiter will search for.

## The ATS-friendly resume format

Use this structure, in this order:

1. **Header**: full name, a short headline (target role and two strengths), phone, email, LinkedIn URL and city.
2. **Professional summary**: three to four lines with your years of experience, domain and two or three quantified results.
3. **Key competencies**: 9 to 12 short skill phrases relevant to the job.
4. **Professional experience**: most recent first, with company, title, dates and 3 to 6 achievement bullets per recent role.
5. **Education**: degree, institution, and grade if it helps.
6. **Certifications and achievements**: only the relevant ones.

### Formatting rules that keep parsers happy

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| One column, left-aligned text | Two-column templates and sidebars |
| Standard headings ("Professional Experience") | Creative headings ("My Journey") |
| Common fonts: Calibri, Arial, Cambria | Icons, skill bars, charts and photos |
| Dates like "Apr 2021 - Present" | Dates in text boxes or mixed formats |
| Contact details in the main body | Contact details only in the page header or footer |
| .docx or a text-based PDF | Scanned PDFs or images of your resume |

A quick test: open your PDF, select all the text and paste it into Notepad. If the text comes out in a sensible order, an ATS can most likely read it too.

## Keywords: match the job, honestly

Recruiters search for the exact names of skills and tools. If the job description says "stakeholder management" and your resume says "worked with various teams", you may not show up.

- Read the job description and list the 10 to 15 skills and requirements it repeats.
- Use the same wording in your summary, competencies and experience bullets, **where it is true for you**.
- Put each important keyword in context. "Led SAP S/4HANA migration for 3 plants" is far stronger than a bare list of tools.

Never add a skill you don't have. Interviews expose it quickly, and it damages trust.

## How long should your resume be?

- Freshers and up to 5 years of experience: **one page**.
- Experienced professionals: **two pages at most**. Keep the last 10 to 15 years in detail and summarise older roles in a line each.

## Checklist before you apply

- Single column, standard headings, no tables or graphics
- Job title in your headline matches the role you're applying for
- The job's key skills appear in your summary and bullets
- Every bullet starts with an action verb and shows a result
- Consistent date format throughout
- File saved as `Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf` or `.docx`
- Two pages or fewer

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PDF or Word better for ATS?
Both work with modern systems as long as the PDF is text-based (created from Word or Google Docs, not scanned). If a portal specifically asks for Word, upload the .docx.

### Do ATS systems reject resumes automatically?
Most systems don't reject resumes on their own; they help recruiters search, filter and rank applicants. A badly parsed or poorly matched resume is simply much less likely to be found or shortlisted.

### Should I use a resume template from Canva?
Many design templates use columns, icons and text boxes that parsers struggle with. They can look good to a person but read poorly to an ATS. A clean single-column layout is safer.

### What is a good ATS score?
Different tools calculate scores differently. Aim to cover most of the job's must-have skills honestly, rather than chasing a particular number.
