You don't need to be technical to get real value from AI. If you can brief a smart colleague, you can use Claude. This guide is for experienced professionals who want to start using it at work this week.

## What Claude is

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. You type (or paste) what you need in plain language, and it writes, summarises, analyses, explains and brainstorms. You can use it on the web at claude.ai or in its desktop and mobile apps. There's a free plan to start.

## How to write a good prompt

Think of it as briefing a capable new team member:

1. **Who you are and the situation**: "I'm the regional sales head for a consumer durables company."
2. **The task**: "Summarise this month's sales review notes for the CEO."
3. **The material**: paste the notes, data or document.
4. **What good looks like**: "One page, three key issues, decisions needed, in a direct tone."
5. **Iterate**: "Shorter." "Add the risks." "Make the second point sharper."

An example of the output you want is often worth more than any clever wording.

## Five ways to use Claude at work this week

**1. Meeting notes into actions**
> Here are my rough notes from today's meeting: [paste]. Turn them into a summary with decisions, owners and deadlines, ready to email.

**2. Make sense of data**
> Here is our monthly data as text: [paste]. What are the three most important trends, what might explain them, and what should we ask next?

**3. Draft proposals and reports**
> Draft a one-page proposal for [project] for [audience] with the problem, solution, cost, risks and the decision needed. My points: [paste].

**4. Better emails**
> Rewrite this email so it's clear, firm and polite, in under 120 words: [paste].

**5. Learn something new fast**
> Explain the basics of [topic] to an experienced manager from [your field], with analogies from my field, then quiz me with 5 questions.

## Your first AI project in one hour

1. **Pick a task** you do every week that takes over an hour: a status report, a sales summary, a candidate shortlist, customer replies.
2. **Write a brief** (10 minutes): the purpose, the audience, what a great result looks like, and one past example.
3. **Run it** (20 minutes) with this week's material, and refine until you'd send it.
4. **Save the prompt** as a template, or set up a Claude Project with your instructions and reference documents, so next week takes ten minutes.
5. **Measure the result**: time saved and quality. That's also a strong line for your resume and your next performance review.

## Using AI responsibly at work

- Follow your company's policy on AI tools and confidential data.
- Don't paste sensitive personal, customer or financial information unless your organisation allows it.
- Check facts, figures and names. AI can be confidently wrong.
- Keep your own judgement in charge. You are the expert.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need coding skills to use Claude?
No. You use Claude by writing instructions in plain English (or other languages). Coding is optional.

### Is Claude safe for work documents?
Follow your employer's policy. Many companies allow AI tools with business accounts and rules about confidential data. When in doubt, remove sensitive details first.

### What is a Claude Project?
A Project lets you save instructions and reference documents in one place, so Claude uses the same context every time you work on that topic.

### I'm over 40. Is it too late to learn AI tools?
Not at all. Experienced professionals often get more from AI because they know exactly what good work looks like and can judge the output quickly.
