Many job seekers in India skip the cover letter, and on many portals nobody reads it. But in the right situations, a short, specific letter is what gets you shortlisted.

## When a cover letter helps

- You're applying **directly** to a company or emailing a hiring manager.
- The role is at a **multinational** or a company with a global hiring process.
- You're applying for a **senior** or leadership role.
- You're **changing careers** and need to explain the move.
- The posting **asks for one**, which is also a test of whether you read instructions.

## The structure

**Paragraph 1: Why this role.** Name the role and what draws you to the company, based on what the job description or their website says.

**Paragraph 2: Proof.** Two or three achievements most relevant to their needs, with numbers.

**Paragraph 3: Fit.** How you meet the key requirements, and how you'd approach the first months.

**Paragraph 4: Close.** A short, confident line inviting a conversation.

Keep it to 250 to 350 words.

## A template you can adapt

> Dear Hiring Manager,
>
> I'm applying for the [Role] position at [Company]. [One specific thing from the job description or company] stood out to me, because [why it matters to you].
>
> In my current role at [Company], I [achievement with a number]. Before that, I [second achievement]. Both required [key skill from the job description], which is central to this role.
>
> You're looking for someone who can [requirement 1] and [requirement 2]. I've done both: [one line of evidence]. In my first months, I'd focus on [one or two priorities].
>
> I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I can help [team or goal]. Thank you for your time.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Your name]

## Mistakes to avoid

- Opening with "I am writing to express my interest in..."
- Repeating your resume line by line.
- Generic praise for the company that could apply anywhere.
- Placeholders left in by mistake, like "[Company]".
- Going over one page.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do Indian companies read cover letters?
Some do, especially for direct applications, multinational roles and senior positions. On high-volume portals, the resume usually matters more.

### How long should a cover letter be?
250 to 350 words, in three or four short paragraphs, fitting comfortably on one page.

### Should I send the cover letter as a file or in the email body?
If you're emailing, put a shorter version in the email body and attach your resume. If a portal has an upload field, attach the letter as a PDF.

### Can I use the same cover letter for every job?
Use the same structure, but tailor the first two paragraphs to each role. Generic letters are easy to spot.
