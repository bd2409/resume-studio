Resume Studio for Claude

Resume Studio's Claude connector checks your resume against a job right inside your Claude chat: a match score out of 100, the keywords you hit and miss, your strengths, and the three fixes that will help most.

Set it up

  1. In Claude, open Settings > Connectors and find Resume Studio in the directory, then select Connect.
  2. Or add it as a custom connector with this URL: {{ cfg.SITE_URL }}/mcp
  3. No account or sign-in is needed.

What to ask

  • "Score my resume for this product manager job" (paste both)
  • "What keywords is my resume missing for this role?"
  • "Help me get a tailored resume for this job"

What it can do

  • Check resume against a job: reads the resume and job text you share in the chat and returns a score, matched and missing keywords, strengths and top fixes.
  • Start a tailored resume: gives you a link to Resume Studio, where you upload your resumes and get a rewritten resume and cover letter in 4 designs as Word and PDF. Your first one is free.

Your data

The resume and job text are sent to our server only to produce your check, and are not stored. We keep a count of checks per day and the score, with no name, email or resume text. See the privacy policy.
