Frequently asked questions

What do I need to get started?

At least one of your existing resumes (PDF or Word) and the full job description you're applying for. Uploading several versions of your resume (up to {{ cfg.MAX_RESUMES }}) helps us find more of your achievements.

Will you add skills or experience I don't have?

No. We only use facts from the resumes you upload. Every line is checked against them before you see it. If the job asks for something your resumes don't mention, we list it as a suggestion so you can add it yourself if it's true.

How long does it take?

Usually two to four minutes after payment. You'll see the progress on screen and we'll also email you a link.

What format will I get?

An editable Microsoft Word (.docx) file and a PDF, both in our clean, ATS-friendly format.

Can I make changes?

Yes. You can edit any line on the review page for {{ cfg.EDIT_WINDOW_DAYS }} days, and regenerate the resume up to {{ cfg.MAX_REGENERATIONS }} times with your own instructions, for example "focus more on team leadership". You can also edit the Word file anytime.

Is my data safe?

Your files are used only to write your resume. They're stored securely and deleted automatically after {{ cfg.RETENTION_DAYS }} days. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. See our privacy policy.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, netbanking and popular wallets, processed securely by Razorpay.

What if something goes wrong?

If your resume can't be generated, we retry automatically. If it still fails, you get a full refund. See our refund policy, or contact us.

I'm applying to several jobs. Do I pay for each?

Yes, each job description gets its own tailored resume for Rs {{ cfg.PRICE_RUPEES }}, because the content is rewritten for each role.
