Refund and cancellation policy

Each resume is created specifically for you as soon as you pay, so we handle refunds as follows.

Full refund

  • If we cannot generate your resume because of a problem on our side.
  • If you were charged more than once for the same order.

Not refundable

  • Once your resume has been delivered, because the work is complete. Instead, use your free edits and regenerations for {{ cfg.EDIT_WINDOW_DAYS }} days, or contact us and we'll help improve it.
  • If the uploaded files are unreadable or not resumes. Contact us and we'll help you re-upload.

How to request a refund

Email {{ cfg.CONTACT_EMAIL }} within 7 days of payment with the email address you used at checkout. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Cancellation

Orders can't be cancelled after payment because generation starts immediately.
